Small kana such as ゅ now stay with the preceding sound across lyric segments. This also covers mixed hiragana and katakana, half-width kana, voiced marks, long vowels, and kana iteration marks.

Kanji endings and inflections are read together. Contractions such as `yomu nda` and `iku nda` stay joined as `yomunda` and `ikunda`. Matching supplied romaji and LRCLIB lyrics get the spacing correction too.

Original punctuation and lyric timing are preserved. Uncertain supplied readings are left alone.
